# Internship and Placement Policy

## Internship duration

An internship must run for a minimum of eight weeks and a maximum of six months.
Internships shorter than eight weeks are not recorded on the transcript.

## Internship approval

Every internship must be approved before it starts. Submit the company name, the
mentor email address, a one-page description of the work and the expected
duration through the placement cell portal. Approval takes three working days.

## Work during internship

An intern is not permitted to write an internal examination while an internship
is in progress. The head of the department must be informed at least one week
before the examination date.

## Placement drives

Eligibility for a placement drive requires a cumulative CGPA of 6.0 with no
backlogs at the time of registration. Final-year students who have joined a job
may still register for drives, but must inform the placement cell in writing
before accepting an offer.

## Referrals

A referral request must be sent through the placement cell, not directly to the
recruiter. Each student may submit a maximum of three referral requests per
recruiter per drive.

## Attendance at drives

Attendance at a placement talk or a written test is recorded. A student who does
not attend a drive they registered for is barred from the next drive for that
recruiter.
