# Library and Resource Centre

## Opening hours

The library is open from 08:00 to 20:00 Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 on
Saturdays, and closed on Sundays and public holidays. During examination weeks
the reading hall stays open until 23:00, and the turnstile gate opens at 07:30.

## Borrowing limits

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books for fourteen days. Final-year
students may borrow six books for twenty-eight days. Books on reserve, marked `R`
on the spine, are for reading inside the library only and cannot be borrowed.

## Renewals

A borrowed book can be renewed twice through the student portal, provided nobody
has reserved it. Renewal fails automatically once the borrowing limit is reached,
so return a book before borrowing a new one.

## Fines

Overdue books cost Rs 2 per day per book, capped at Rs 100 per book. Books lost
must be replaced within thirty days; if the title is unavailable, the department
charges the average retail price plus a Rs 50 processing fee.

## Reference material

Encyclopaedias, dictionaries, standards and atlas volumes are kept in the
reference section and are not issued at any time. Photocopying is available on
the first floor; Rs 2 per page.
