# Residence Life: Visitor and Access Rules

## Visitor hours

Visitors are signed in at the gate between 07:00 and 21:00. Outside those hours the
gate is closed and no visitor may enter the campus, including on public holidays.
Each visitor must be registered by a resident before arrival, and the resident is
responsible for collecting the visitor pass from the security desk.

## Curfew

Residents must be inside their block by 23:30 on weekdays and by 01:00 on weekends.
Security records entry time automatically through the turnstile system. Repeated
late entry results in a written warning from the warden.

## Prohibited items

The following are not permitted anywhere inside the residence blocks: induction
cookers, immersion rods, LPG cylinders, extension boards with more than four
sockets, and any animal other than registered service animals.

## Room change requests

Room changes are considered only at the start of a semester. Submit the request
through the residence portal with a reason. Requests are granted when a room in
the requested block is vacant; the student pays the difference in room rent.

## Electrical repairs

Do not attempt any electrical repair yourself. Raise a ticket from the block
office or the residence portal. Emergency faults, including a burning smell,
sparking, or a tripped main breaker, must be reported to the security desk by
telephone immediately.
